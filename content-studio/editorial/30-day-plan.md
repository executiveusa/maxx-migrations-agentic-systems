# Northwest Field Notes — 30-Day Editorial Coverage Plan

Created: 2026-08-10

This is an editorial backlog for nontechnical business owners. The dates below are **coverage dates**, not publication dates. Nothing in this plan is represented as historically published. Every item remains a draft until human review and a real release date.

## Editorial rule

Write for the owner who has a business problem but does not want to become a software administrator. Answer the main question early. Use plain language. Give one practical next step. Do not invent results, savings, customer claims, publication history, vendor capabilities or statistics.
