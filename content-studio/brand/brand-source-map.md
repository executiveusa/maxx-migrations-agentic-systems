# MACS Blog Brand Source Map

## Approved visual sources

- MACS logo: owner-provided PNG supplied 2026-08-10.
- Founder photography: owner-provided waterfront photograph of Stacy and Stavari supplied 2026-08-10.
- Public marketing shell: `apps/maxx-web/app/globals.css` marketing tokens and current founder-led homepage.
- Typography: existing `font-display` and body typography in the host application.
- CTA language/style: current `$497 Vibe Audit` buttons and restrained rounded treatment.

## Blog adaptation

The blog must feel native to MACS rather than like a generic CMS template:

- warm paper background;
- evergreen hero/surfaces;
- charcoal text;
- muted green/slate support colors;
- large editorial headings;
- real photography and simple custom diagrams;
- Pacific Northwest atmosphere through color, topography and local language—not pine-tree clip art or generic mountain stock photos.

## Motion

Use small, interruptible reveals and hover lift only. Respect reduced-motion preference. Do not use scroll-jacking, long parallax, auto-advancing carousels or decorative motion that competes with reading.

## Locked host boundary

Blog work may reuse host tokens and public components, but it must not replace or rewrite the authenticated app design system.
