# MACS Company Truth for Editorial Use

## Verified and safe to use

- MACS Digital Media is positioned as a Pacific Northwest father-and-son technology company.
- MACS is designed for nontechnical business owners.
- Stacy is the nontechnical founder perspective that grounds the product in plain-language usability.
- Stavari is positioned around Shopify, e-commerce and UGC and is the owner of PostaTees.
- The public first step is the $497 Vibe Audit.
- MACS uses the principle: diagnose before prescribing.
- MACS may recommend keeping existing technology when a larger replacement is not justified.
- The offer ladder includes Vibe Audit, Vibe Rescue, Sovereign Launch and MAXX Migrations.
- MAXX Migrations is positioned for businesses that want to migrate away from expensive platforms or fragmented stacks and finish with training and a documented handoff.
- MACS emphasizes owner control, portability and optional ongoing support rather than dependence.
- ASC3ND and MACS Client Zero are the currently approved proof stories on the public site.

## Requires evidence before publication

- Any savings amount or percentage.
- Any conversion, revenue, lead or time-saving result.
- Any customer testimonial or quote.
- Any claim that a third-party platform prevents export or ownership.
- Any current third-party product feature, price, API or migration capability.
- Any exact founder biography detail not supplied or approved by the owner.
- Any broad claim about what most business owners experience.

## Prohibited as invented content

- Fake publication dates.
- Fake case studies.
- Fake customer counts.
- Fake awards or certifications.
- Fake performance metrics.
- Fake local offices or service locations.
- Fake comparisons presented as tested results.

## Editorial voice

Write like one business owner explaining a useful fix to another. Use ordinary words, short sections, direct answers and practical next actions. Technical detail may be added after the reader understands why it matters.
