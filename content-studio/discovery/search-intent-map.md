# Northwest Field Notes — Search Intent Map

## 1. Reduce software spend
Reader questions:
- What subscriptions should I cancel?
- Do I need another CRM?
- What does an AI agent really cost?

Desired action: inventory the current stack before buying another tool.

## 2. Fix disconnected systems
Reader questions:
- Why do leads disappear between my website and follow-up?
- Should I use an all-in-one platform or connected tools?
- Why does automation create more work?

Desired action: map one real workflow and find the first broken handoff.

## 3. Use AI without becoming technical
Reader questions:
- What is the difference between a chatbot, agent and automation?
- What should AI be allowed to do?
- What should I organize before adding AI?

Desired action: choose one business job, evidence requirement and authority boundary before choosing a tool.

## 4. E-commerce without the mystery
Reader questions:
- What makes a new Shopify store feel trustworthy?
- Do I need UGC or polished creative?
- What belongs in a simple UGC brief?

Desired action: improve the product story and one conversion path before buying more traffic.

## 5. Own and migrate the system
Reader questions:
- What should I be able to export?
- What do I own after an agency engagement?
- How do I leave GoHighLevel without losing workflows and business memory?

Desired action: document data, workflows, credentials, ownership and recovery before migration.

## 6. Pacific Northwest operator intent
Reader questions:
- What should a Seattle-area small business clean up before AI?
- What should a Washington business own before hiring a tech partner?
- How should a PNW nonprofit organize mission knowledge before automation?

Desired action: use local context only when it adds real decision value; do not create doorway pages or fake local claims.
