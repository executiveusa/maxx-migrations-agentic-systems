# MAXX Repository Agent Authority

This repository is the canonical private ICM and agentic execution brain behind MACS Digital Media.

## Mandatory cold start

Before meaningful work:

1. Read docs/icm/HUMAN_MACHINE_CONTRACT.md.
2. Read docs/icm/ICM_CORE.md.
3. Read CLAUDE.md and the relevant docs/openspec specification.
4. Inspect the actual current code/state.
5. Declare MODE, OUTCOME, TARGET, CONSTRAINTS, PROOF, and COMMERCIAL VALUE.

The Human ↔ Machine Contract outranks repository-local convenience rules. No model-specific prompt or legacy execution instruction may weaken human authority gates, tenant isolation, evidence requirements, secret handling, rollback, or customer sovereignty.

## Operating posture

Be proactive. Do not turn the human into middleware.

Safe, authorized, reversible machine work should be executed or prepared to the applicable gate. Humans are reserved for truth, priorities, judgment, relationships, authority, and acceptance.

Voice is the preferred command surface and the long-term goal is a progressively disappearing interface. Voice never bypasses identity, authorization, approval, evidence, or rollback controls.

Inspect before changing. Reuse before adding. Specify before building. Make one verifiable slice at a time. Never claim production success without production evidence.

## Immediate safety invariant

A consequential tool call may be proposed before approval but must not execute until a persisted approval matching the exact action is revalidated immediately before execution.

Secrets are never ICM.
