## Design observations from actual screenshot views

- Feast homepage: near-black field, high-contrast orange CTA, giant sales-target statement and animated-looking wireframe restaurant visual. **Keep mechanic:** clear single conversion and transparent goal. **Never copy:** branding, visual treatment, exact price/ROI claim or 3D art.
- Remy hero: dark collage of real restaurant/creator footage; one visible primary purchase CTA; price and owner audience prominent. **Keep mechanic:** immediate audience and job clarity. **Never copy:** collage, Remy name/logo, orange style or unlimited claims.
- Revenue analyzer: simple problem/benefit framing, explicit POS/logos illustrated around central data point and one primary CTA. **Keep mechanic:** input → insight → action. **Avoid:** logos for unconnected providers or unsupported machine-learning claims.
- Remy application tab: Applications / Texts / Content tabs with counts, filter/sort, creator metrics and large Approve/Deny controls. The 20/4/2 and social metrics are screenshot example data, **not MACS data**. No copy into live UI.
- Remy content review: status lanes Content Review, Approved Content, Running as Ads; creator cards and paid/pending indicators; models state transitions.
- Remy recruitment ads: step completion marker, ad status and actual campaign spend/click/app metrics; status such as Paused on Meta distinct from queued or live.
- Remy availability: recurring slots with edit/delete and separate calendar; appropriate time zone and maximum capacity still need independent spec.
- Remy creative brief: editable restaurant/context/feature and restrictions, separate program food-credit, creator payout and applicant requirements.
- Remy messaging: after-approval visit confirmations, brief link, pass link, reminder, STOP opt-out. **Do not claim autonomous communication** without consent, carrier verification and human-approved message templates.
- Remy check-in: staff-visible pass proof and visit outcome, not merely booked slots. Visit does not establish delivered content or downstream sales.

## Reference ethics and verification

- Method: `replica-recon` public-state analysis, source-first; not a pixel clone. `morluto/rea` binary/runtime extraction is inapplicable and intentionally unused.
- Do not claim source pages prove the behavior of an authenticated Remy account. The product images are curated examples, not independent test evidence.
- Public marketing/SEO pages may have dynamic experiments, pricing or moving screenshots. Record date/time for every comparison.
- Later review must use owner-controlled account only where contractually permitted; no bypassing login, harvesting private API traffic or automating competitor dashboards.
