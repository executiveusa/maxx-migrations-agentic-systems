# MAXX Brand Architecture

**Status:** OWNER LOCK — 2026-08-10

## Decision

MACS Digital Media is the company and human relationship. MAXX is the customer-facing product universe.

The brand should not position itself in the middle of the market. High-touch MACS/MAXX work is premium, diagnosis-led and ownership-first. Lower-friction revenue comes from repeatable MAXX digital products that solve one clear problem without requiring a custom engagement.

## Brand hierarchy

### MACS Digital Media
The Pacific Northwest father-and-son company.

Public role:
- trust;
- diagnosis;
- human partnership;
- custom implementation;
- training and handoff.

### MAXX
The product family and memorable public product identity.

MAXX may appear across agents, tools, migrations, editorial content and repeatable digital products.

### Agent MAXX
A separate product: the MAXX AI avatar/operator.

Owner decision:
- intended to become a downloadable agent;
- intended to have a free release;
- designed for nontechnical owners;
- may lead users into additional MAXX products or MACS services;
- must not be advertised as downloadable until a verified release artifact exists.

The canonical Agent MAXX avatar already exists outside the current marketing repo. Do not invent, redraw or replace it. Use a reserved identity panel until the approved asset is supplied.

### MAXX Notes
Editorial brand.

**Name:** MAXX Notes  
**Subtitle:** Notes from the Northwest

Purpose:
- explain technical problems in plain English;
- help nontechnical owners reduce software waste;
- show practical AI beyond chatbot use;
- teach ownership, portability and safe automation;
- cover Shopify/e-commerce/UGC through Stavari's operator perspective;
- create useful search and social content from real customer pain;
- earn trust before a sale.

### MAXX Products / MAXX Drops
Self-serve product layer.

Product rule:
- one recognizable problem;
- one understandable outcome;
- repeatable delivery;
- low or near-zero custom labor after the product is mature;
- free or paid depending on the product;
- useful enough to stand alone;
- premium presentation even when free.

Potential formats:
- downloadable agents;
- starter systems;
- operator kits;
- checklists;
- templates;
- small diagnostic tools;
- workflow packs;
- narrowly scoped digital utilities.

Do not publish a product as available until its delivery path is verified.

### MAXX Migrations
Flagship high-value transformation.

Target:
Businesses trapped inside expensive all-in-one platforms or fragmented stacks that want an owned replacement.

Commercial promise:
- diagnose first;
- inventory and extract what matters;
- redesign around the actual business;
- rebuild and migrate;
- train the owner/team;
- document the system;
- hand over the keys;
- offer extended MACS support as optional partnership rather than technical captivity.

Payment may eventually support full payment or a documented payment plan, but final commercial terms require a separate approved pricing decision.

### Hermes
Runtime infrastructure under the hood.

Hermes is not the primary customer-facing brand. MAXX wraps runtime capability in business context, permissions, workflows, evidence and a product experience.

## Commercial architecture

Two visible lanes:

### 1. MAXX Products
Free and one-off digital value.

Goal:
- generate trust;
- create low-friction revenue;
- help MACS learn from smaller customers;
- create reusable IP;
- avoid custom-service drag.

### 2. MACS Partnership
High-touch diagnosis and transformation.

Current path:

`$497 Vibe Audit → bounded rescue / sovereign build → MAXX Migrations when justified → optional ongoing partnership`

MAXX Migrations remains the premium anchor. The site must not use fake crossed-out pricing or claim enterprise readiness that has not been proven.

## Positioning rule

Do not compete as a generic middle-market AI agency.

Premium work should feel selective, diagnostic and ownership-led. Smaller MAXX products should feel like the accessible entry objects in a premium brand: useful, intentional and well-made, not cheap services disguised as products.

## Current proof boundary

Current source truth still requires live proof for durable tenant isolation, approval enforcement and other Client Zero runtime claims before stronger production statements are made.

Do not let MAXX brand theater outrun verified capability.

## Historical conflict resolution

A July 2026 MAXX brief packaged MAXX itself primarily as a managed AI operating-system service with specific package pricing. That document remains useful product history, but this owner decision supersedes that public brand architecture:

- MACS = company / partnership;
- MAXX = product universe;
- Agent MAXX = free downloadable product direction;
- MAXX Notes = editorial;
- MAXX Products = repeatable free/paid digital shelf;
- MAXX Migrations = premium backend transformation.

Older package prices and promises are not automatically approved for the current public site.

## Design rule

Homepage stays founder-led and pain-first.

MAXX product pages may use more cinematic/dark visual language, but they must preserve:
- plain-English outcomes;
- restrained motion;
- mobile-first behavior;
- clear availability states;
- real proof;
- human approval and ownership principles.
