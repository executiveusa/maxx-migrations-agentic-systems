# Feature Page Copy

Source of truth for `/features/*`, rendered by
`apps/maxx-web/components/features/FeaturePageLayout.tsx` with per-page
props in `apps/maxx-web/app/features/*/page.tsx`.

## /features/community
- **Title:** Community & Courses
- **Description:** Give your volunteers, donors, and staff one place to
  post updates, ask questions, message each other directly, and complete
  onboarding courses — without renting a separate community platform.
- **App CTA:** Open Community → `/app/community`

## /features/courses
- **Title:** Courses
- **Description:** Turn your volunteer onboarding, donor stewardship
  training, board orientation, and grant readiness knowledge into
  structured courses with modules, lessons, and progress tracking.
- **App CTA:** Open Courses → `/app/community/courses`

## /features/workflows
- **Title:** Workflow Builder
- **Description:** Build automations as a readable, ordered list of steps
  — trigger, condition, wait, send, notify, approve — instead of dragging
  boxes around an infinite canvas.
- **App CTA:** Open Workflow Builder → `/app/workflows`

## /features/social-planner
- **Title:** Social Media Planner
- **Description:** Plan, draft, and schedule posts to your Facebook Page
  and Instagram Business account from a single content calendar, with
  campaign templates built for mission-driven storytelling.
- **App CTA:** Open Social Planner → `/app/social-planner`

## /features/ghl-import
- **Title:** GHL Import Wizard
- **Description:** Leave GoHighLevel without losing your data. Map every
  contact, pipeline, opportunity, note, task, and tag into your owned CRM
  with a validation report before anything runs.
- **App CTA:** Open GHL Import → `/app/import/ghl`

## /features/missed-call-text-back
- **Title:** Missed Call Text Back
- **Description:** Every missed call automatically triggers a text reply
  within seconds, so a family calling about food access — or a donor
  calling about a gift — never hits a dead end.
- **App CTA:** Open Missed Calls → `/app/missed-calls`

## /features/website-migration
- **Title:** Website Migration Engine
- **Description:** We crawl your existing site, inventory every page and
  asset, rewrite the copy in your voice, and rebuild it on an owned,
  sovereign component system — with a full task timeline you can watch in
  real time.
- **App CTA:** Open Migrations → `/app/migrations`

Every page also carries a secondary CTA to `/migration-audit` and three
highlight cards specific to that feature — see the individual page files
for exact wording, which stays in sync with this document.
