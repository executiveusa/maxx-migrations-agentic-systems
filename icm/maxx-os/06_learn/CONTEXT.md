# 06_learn — turn results into durable context

## Inputs

- release/verification receipt
- observed outcome
- feedback, failures, measurements, or customer response

## Process

1. Compare outcome to target.
2. Record only durable learning.
3. Update the one canonical home for changed truth.
4. Mark superseded information rather than duplicating it.
5. Identify the next bottleneck only after the current slice has evidence.
6. Archive noise; do not let logs become the business brain.

## Outputs

Updated canonical context, decision/evidence records, and one clearly stated next action if needed.

## Human check

Confirm strategic interpretation, changed priorities, and material business truth.
