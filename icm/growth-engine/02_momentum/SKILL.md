# Momentum

## Public outcome

**Stay visible. Turn attention into opportunity.**

Momentum is for a business that has something worth buying but does not yet create enough consistent qualified demand or follow-up.

## Money condition

Start here when:

- the offer is real but lead flow is inconsistent
- existing customers or relationships are underused
- content has no clear commercial path
- good attention dies without follow-up
- referrals happen by accident
- the team is posting more than it is learning
- the business is adding channels before mastering one

## Full reduction

Write four lines only:

1. **Audience:** who should notice?
2. **Outcome:** what useful result should they want?
3. **Proof:** why should they pay attention now?
4. **Call:** what single action moves them forward?

## Acquisition order

Use the cheapest credible path first:

1. warm outreach
2. referrals
3. useful public content
4. cold outreach
5. paid advertising

This ordering is a MACS operating default, not a claim that every business must use every channel.

## More -> Better -> New

Before adding a new channel:

1. **More:** can the winning activity simply be done more often?
2. **Better:** what bottleneck is lowering response, qualification, or conversion?
3. **New:** only after the first two are economically exhausted, test a new placement, platform, or acquisition method.

## Lead magnet rule

A lead magnet should solve one narrow problem the right customer already cares about.

It should:

- attract the right person
- deliver real value quickly
- be easy to consume
- reveal the logical next problem
- contain one clear next action

Do not create a lead magnet because a funnel template says to have one.

## Primary metrics

- engaged leads
- qualified opportunities
- booked calls / appointments
- show rate
- close rate
- customer acquisition cost
- revenue and gross profit by channel when attributable
- referral rate
- response / reply rate
- time from first engagement to next useful action

Reach, impressions, followers, likes, and views are diagnostic metrics, not the commercial finish line.

## Story/content filter

Every piece of commercial content should do at least one job:

- reveal a problem
- demonstrate proof
- teach something useful
- show the work
- answer a real objection
- make the next step clearer

If it does none of these, question why it exists.

## Stop conditions

Momentum is working when qualified demand is sufficiently repeatable that the next constraint moves downstream into delivery, capacity, owner dependence, or system complexity. At that point, route to Scale.
