# Scale

## Public outcome

**The idea is working. Grow without complexity.**

Scale is for a business that has proof the idea works. The problem is no longer whether people want it. The problem is producing more of the result without making the owner the permanent bottleneck.

## Money condition

Start here when:

- demand exceeds current capacity
- delivery quality varies as volume rises
- the owner is required in too many routine decisions
- knowledge lives in people instead of a repeatable process
- every new customer creates proportional manual work
- the team keeps solving the same operational problem again
- tools are being added before the underlying process is understood

## Full reduction

Write four lines only:

1. **Working path:** what already produces value?
2. **Constraint:** what prevents doing more of it cleanly?
3. **Outcome:** what should grow without growing complexity at the same rate?
4. **Metric:** what proves leverage increased?

## Scale sequence

1. Isolate the working path.
2. Remove unnecessary variation.
3. Define the repeatable service/process.
4. Document the minimum instructions another capable person needs.
5. Test handoff without founder rescue.
6. Quantify inputs, outputs, cycle time, errors, margin, and owner intervention.
7. Fix the system where handoff fails.
8. Add software or automation only where the process is understood and the leverage is measurable.
9. Increase volume.
10. Repeat.

## Productization rule

Do not scale custom chaos.

Prefer a named, teachable, repeatable method that produces a valuable result. Keep human judgment where it creates value; standardize what does not need to be reinvented every time.

## Owner-dependence test

For each recurring task ask:

- Must the owner do this?
- Must a human do this?
- Must it be done at all?
- Can the decision rule be written down?
- Can another person reproduce the result from the instructions and evidence?

When the owner is pulled back into routine execution, diagnose the system instead of treating founder heroics as the permanent fix.

## Primary metrics

- revenue / gross profit per owner hour
- throughput per team member
- cycle time
- gross margin
- rework / error rate
- owner intervention rate
- percentage of delivery completed from documented process
- capacity before quality degradation
- recurring revenue / repeat demand when relevant
- conversion consistency across more than one seller/operator

## Story pattern

- What was already working?
- Where did growth begin to create strain?
- What was made repeatable?
- What changed in capacity, speed, margin, consistency, or owner time?
- What became possible next?

## Stop conditions

Scale is working when increased volume no longer requires equivalent increases in founder effort or unmanaged complexity, and the operating result remains measurable and repeatable.
