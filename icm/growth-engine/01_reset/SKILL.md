# Reset

## Public outcome

**Fix what is getting in the way. Make the path clear again.**

Reset is for a business that already has demand, customers, reputation, traffic, or useful systems, but value is leaking before it turns into action or money.

## Money condition

Start here when the closest constraint to revenue is one of these:

- broken or confusing website path
- weak trust or proof
- slow contact or follow-up
- disconnected tools or vendors
- duplicated subscriptions
- missing ownership or access
- unclear offer or CTA
- preventable errors or manual rework

Do not add more traffic to a broken path.

## Full reduction

Write four lines only:

1. **Condition:** what is broken or leaking?
2. **Outcome:** what should happen instead?
3. **Metric:** what proves recovery?
4. **Call:** what is the smallest next action?

Mechanisms belong in implementation notes, not the promise.

## Lowest-hanging-fruit order

1. Revenue-blocking errors.
2. Broken contact / checkout / booking / lead capture.
3. Trust and proof gaps.
4. Offer and CTA ambiguity.
5. Slow handoffs and follow-up.
6. Duplicate or unnecessary systems/costs.
7. Cosmetic improvements with no demonstrated commercial effect.

## Execution loop

1. Capture baseline evidence.
2. Map the customer path from arrival to desired action.
3. Identify the highest-value point of friction.
4. Fix only that slice.
5. Verify on real devices / real workflow.
6. Measure recovery.
7. Continue to the next constraint only if the first is no longer dominant.

## Primary metrics

Use the smallest set that matches the problem:

- completion rate
- lead/contact conversion
- booked qualified opportunities
- sales conversion
- checkout / form error rate
- response time
- cycle time
- owner hours required
- recurring software/vendor cost removed
- revenue or gross profit recovered, when attributable

## Story pattern

- What was getting in the way?
- What did that cost in action, trust, time, or money?
- What changed?
- What evidence shows the path works now?
- What can the business do next that it could not do cleanly before?

Do not dramatize ordinary cleanup. Evidence carries the story.

## Stop conditions

Reset is complete when:

- the primary conversion path works
- ownership/access is clear
- the main offer and next action are understandable
- critical follow-up works
- the highest-value leak is no longer the dominant constraint

Then route to Momentum, Scale, or Launch based on the new condition.
