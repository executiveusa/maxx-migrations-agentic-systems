# MACS Four-Bucket Growth Engine

## Prime rule

Public strategy has four buckets only:

- Reset
- Momentum
- Scale
- Launch

Do not create a fifth public category. Internal labels such as Client Zero, R&D, Built Here, proof, lab, or product names can exist, but they must support one of the four outcomes or remain internal.

Read `docs/icm/MONEY_MODELS.md` before commercial decisions.

## Route first

Choose the bucket from the present business condition, not from the service we want to sell.

- **Reset**: something valuable is leaking, broken, scattered, untrusted, slow, or confusing.
- **Momentum**: the offer exists; the problem is attention, consistency, distribution, follow-up, or qualified demand.
- **Scale**: the idea works; the constraint is owner dependence, throughput, repeatability, complexity, or capacity.
- **Launch**: the thing is new; the constraint is market proof, first demand, first customers, or first repeatable acquisition path.

If two buckets apply, start with the one that blocks money closest to the customer today.

## Three passes every agent must run

### Pass 1 — Full reduction

Reduce the problem and promise until a nontechnical owner can repeat them.

Output:

- Current condition: one sentence.
- Desired outcome: one sentence.
- Primary metric: one line.
- Primary CTA: one line.

Delete mechanism words unless the buyer needs them to decide.

Examples of mechanism words to hide by default: AI, agentic, model, orchestration, automation stack, MCP, RAG, API, vector, workflow engine.

### Pass 2 — Full call

Define the single next action that advances the commercial state.

A call must be:

- specific
- low-friction
- measurable
- appropriate to the visitor's awareness
- connected to a real owner/team follow-up process

Default CTA: `Tell us what's important.`

Do not create multiple equal CTAs on one decision surface.

### Pass 3 — Collins-level storytelling

Tell only enough story to create understanding, trust, and movement.

Use:

1. tension
2. decision
3. work that mattered
4. evidence
5. next possibility

Then run a Humanizer pass:

- remove inflated language
- remove formulaic contrasts
- remove filler
- remove generic positive endings
- remove repeated explanations
- remove unsupported claims
- replace abstract nouns with concrete consequences where possible

## Experiment loop

Every material recommendation becomes a test unless direct evidence already settles it.

1. Baseline.
2. Pick the smallest high-value constraint.
3. Form one hypothesis.
4. Change one meaningful variable or one tightly coupled bundle.
5. Run long enough to observe real behavior.
6. Record spend, owner time, leads, qualified opportunities, conversion, revenue/profit when available, and time to first value.
7. Kill, keep, improve, or scale.
8. Store the result under `icm/growth-engine/experiments/`.

Use `experiments/TEMPLATE.md`.

## Commercial evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. cash / gross profit
2. closed sales
3. qualified pipeline
4. engaged leads / booked calls
5. conversion behavior
6. retention / repeat use
7. owner time saved
8. attention metrics

Attention metrics alone are not a win.

## Human gates

Require owner approval before:

- pricing changes
- guarantees
- public claims
- paid spend
- bulk outbound
- contracts
- publishing customer names/assets
- deleting production data
- consequential external actions

The agent can prepare the work up to the gate without turning the owner into middleware.
