## Matt Plapp — verified source

Source: Seven Figure Agency, `Matt Plapp Case Study: Restaurant Marketing That Works Held Churn to 1.9%`, https://sevenfigureagency.com/case-studies/matt-plapp/ . Retrieved 2026-09-07. The page identifies its interview as recorded in June 2021. Source type: agency-hosted interview/case study; outcomes are self-reported, not independently audited.

The interview describes a 2015 National Pretzel Day promotion at three restaurants: three Facebook ads costing $50 and three emails, with $18,000 in reported trackable sales in one day. It also describes the subsequent funnel work: attract attention, collect contact information, present an offer, and encourage a return visit. The page reports 300+ restaurant locations served, 1.9% year-to-date revenue churn and a 38% company profit margin. These are historical claims from the interview/page, not current MACS or Ubuntu results. Do not use the promotion as a forecast or imply that the spend was the sole cause of sales.

Transferable mechanics: narrow restaurant specialization, reuse of a working delivery process, measurable offers, first-party customer relationships and repeat visits. Psychology inference: a specific relevant offer reduces uncertainty and creates a reason to act; familiarity and a useful return invitation may support repeat behavior. This is a MACS interpretation, not a quoted or scientifically validated proprietary Plapp framework.
