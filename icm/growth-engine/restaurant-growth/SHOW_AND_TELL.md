# Show and Tell™ — Monthly Restaurant Content

Status: PROPOSED operating procedure. The name is owner-selected; ™ does not establish registration. The cadence is adapted from MACS's existing ASC3ND system, not a new posting strategy.

## The rhythm

- Monday — Who we are: food, culture, people and identity.
- Wednesday — Let me show you: a real story, preparation, kitchen or person.
- Friday — Here's what you can do: visit, order, catering inquiry, event or authentic community action.

Three primary posts per week, four weeks, twelve primary posts. Supporting stories and reuse are optional; do not manufacture a daily posting obligation. Every post has one dominant message and one appropriate next action.

## Monthly capture

The normal plan is one substantial shoot each month. Use two or three shorter visits in the first week only when necessary for different service periods, interviews, catering or owner availability. The owner approves access and timing. A human shooter captures real activity; agents prepare the brief and shot list.

### Before the shoot — agent prepares

Read the client's approved identity, menu, offers, operating hours, permissions, content inventory and last month's evidence. Select the month's one commercial priority. Prepare the twelve-slot calendar, interview questions, shot list, release checklist and missing-facts list. Confirm which dishes and people can be filmed. Do not invent a promotion or ask the owner to create a story that is not real.

### Capture brief — human shooter

Capture clean vertical 9:16 footage with enough framing room for crops and captions; record clean interview audio, ambient sound and useful natural light. Capture horizontal/still alternatives when useful. Film food preparation, grill, finished dishes, service, environment, team, genuine interactions and catering only when available. Record short owner/staff interviews about actual food, culture, work and customer questions. Film customers only with appropriate permission and releases; never stage a testimonial as spontaneous.

A planning target is four identity posts, four story posts and four action posts, with alternate openings, detail shots, stills and reusable B-roll. Capture quantities are a production target, not a guarantee of twelve usable posts. The editor must report gaps rather than invent missing footage.

Useful interview prompts: What are we making? Why is this dish important? What should a first-time guest know? What is happening in the kitchen today? What do customers ask about? What does catering look like here? What would you like people to understand about the business? Ask follow-ups naturally; preserve the speaker's own words.

### Ingest — agent job

Store original assets in the approved owner-controlled storage. Preserve originals, capture date, location, speaker, consent/release reference, dish/subject, rights, orientation, duration and source checksum. Transcribe and tag. Flag uncertain names, claims, permissions or audio. Do not place private customer footage or contact data in public Git. Return an editable media inventory to the editor.

### Edit — editor + agents

Agents prepare selects, transcript-based cuts, captions, draft copy, alternate openings and approved visual templates using existing video capabilities. The human editor selects the real story, checks pacing, sound, image quality and authenticity, and accepts the finished media. Do not generate fake people, customers, food results or testimonial dialogue. Keep source-to-output provenance and reversible project files.

### Review and publish

One approval packet contains the twelve proposed posts, media previews, captions, intended channels, dates, offers, links and exact CTA. Agent QA checks facts, rights, consent, spelling, dates, links, brand, captions and accessibility. The owner approves consequential public material and any offer/spend. Persist approval against the exact payload and revalidate before publishing. Changed claims, media or offers require renewed approval.

Use the existing Postiz MAXX Social capability as the candidate scheduler. Confirm the actual connected account, supported platform, API/CLI permissions and publication receipt before enabling delivery. No direct posting bypass, fake success, or fabricated integration. On failure, preserve the approved asset and retry safely only when authorization remains valid; otherwise return an exception.

### Learn

Verify each published URL and record actual publication time. Capture relevant engagement as diagnostic evidence and follow the primary business metric from the linked experiment. Weekly, prepare a short status and exceptions. Monthly, report what happened, what did not, what was learned and the next recommended action. Use the evidence to prepare the next shoot rather than asking the owner to invent another month's content.

## Human / machine boundary

Machine: research, brief, organizing, transcription, drafts, editing assistance, QA, scheduling preparation, routine monitoring and reporting. Human: physical capture, consent where required, material truth, creative acceptance, consequential approval, sensitive relationships and exceptions. Safe authorized repetitive work should not be returned to the owner as clerical tasks.

## Required proof before activation

Demonstrate one approved asset moving through the existing Agent MAXX → control plane → canonical backend → Postiz adapter → platform → receipt path, including a truthful failure case and approval rejection. Record exact revisions, deployment, tenant scope and evidence. Until then this procedure is PROPOSED, not a production-ready automation.
