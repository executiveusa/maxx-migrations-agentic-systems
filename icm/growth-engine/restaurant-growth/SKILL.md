# MACS Restaurant Growth

Status: PROPOSED — documentation, not a verified live automation. Read `CONTEXT.md` first. This is a niche workflow, not a fifth public service.

## Prime rule

Start with the owner's situation, not a list of deliverables. Use Reset, Momentum, Scale or Launch according to `docs/icm/MONEY_MODELS.md`. Prefer existing demand, existing leads, existing tools and one repeatable job. Investment follows evidence. Do not sell the full machine first unless the client has evidence and urgency.

## 00 — Outcome and authority

Record MODE, OUTCOME, TARGET, CONSTRAINTS, PROOF, COMMERCIAL VALUE, AUTHORITY and ROLLBACK. Resolve the authenticated organization and canonical client context. Inspect existing systems and owner priorities before proposing changes. Never request credentials in chat or store secrets in ICM.

## 01 — Discover

Inspect public business identity, website, menu, ordering, catering, local search, reviews and social. Ask the owner only for material unknowns: desired outcome, capacity, margins, system access, existing customer/lead records, permissions, current follow-up and approved spending. Separate FACT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, DECISION and EVIDENCE. Public ratings and follower counts are not internal revenue data.

## 02 — Diagnose and rank

Identify the problem closest to money. Check existing acquired leads, missed inquiries, repeat customers, conversion and available capacity before buying attention. Use the existing priority heuristic only to rank hypotheses, not forecast ROI. Choose one primary metric, one smallest useful intervention, one spend cap and one owner-time cap. Instantiate `../experiments/TEMPLATE.md` in the approved client-scoped context.

Possible first tests: repair an existing inquiry path; follow up eligible unworked catering inquiries; test a permissioned customer-return offer; improve the visibility and clarity of an existing catering offer. None is automatically appropriate for every restaurant.

## 03 — Propose

Present one condition, one desired outcome, one proof basis and one next action. Keep the complete evidence behind the reduced client view. Apply the existing Art of Reduction skill at R1 by removing duplication without rewriting approved words; humanization is a later approved pass. One dominant idea/action per page. Use the internal Meaning, Clarity, Humanity, Economy and Fit rubric; it is not a clinical ADHD score. Missing facts, consent, accessibility or approval controls are automatic failures.

## 04 — Execute the smallest test

Prepare the work digitally. Reuse the restaurant's own systems before new subscriptions. For customer reactivation, distinguish previous customers from unconverted inquiries; verify lawful permissions, available channels, suppression and opt-out handling before any outreach. Do not recommend SMS or automated calls by default. For catering, verify the offer, capacity, margin, inquiry destination, response ownership and booking evidence. No fabricated offer, testimonial, scarcity, customer result or guaranteed return.

Agents may research, draft, configure in a safe environment, test, organize and prepare approved actions. Humans confirm material truth, priorities, relationships and consequential authority. Persist exact approvals and revalidate immediately before consequential execution. A changed payload requires a new approval.

## 05 — Show and Tell™

Use `SHOW_AND_TELL.md`. Preserve the existing ASC3ND Monday/Wednesday/Friday rhythm. A monthly capture creates reusable authentic media. Agents handle preparation, ingestion, organization, transcription, drafting, editing assistance, QA, scheduling preparation and measurement. Human capture and consequential publication approvals remain explicit. Postiz is the candidate delivery adapter, not the canonical brain. Verify its actual connected runtime and platform permissions before claiming automation works.

## 06 — Measure and learn

Record baseline, change, spend, owner time, qualified opportunities, sales, revenue/gross profit when known, repeat use and time saved when relevant. Use the experiment's primary metric to decide KILL, KEEP, IMPROVE or SCALE. Attention metrics are diagnostic. Do not infer incremental revenue from views or count platform-reported attributed revenue as independently proven incrementality. Preserve sources, date windows and uncertainty.

## 07 — Handoff and next action

Update canonical client context and experiment evidence, identify the next constraint, and leave one clear next action. Record what was tested, what failed, what requires approval and how to recover. The next agent must be able to resume without chat history. A documented workflow is not a live workflow: require actual surface → handler → transport → canonical owner → observable result → evidence motion before a walk-test PASS.
