## Commercial question

- Current condition: The public site contains overlapping service language and proof labels that make the commercial model harder to repeat than necessary.
- Target customer: Owner-led businesses that need help with the digital side of the business.
- Problem closest to money: A visitor may understand individual capabilities without quickly understanding which starting point fits their current condition.
- Desired outcome: Visitors can recognize one of four starting conditions and take one clear next step.
- Baseline: Capture before launch from current analytics and inquiry data; no baseline value is invented here.

## Hypothesis

If we reduce the homepage and Work taxonomy to exactly **Reset, Momentum, Scale, Launch** and keep one primary CTA, then qualified inquiry behavior will become easier to interpret and improve because visitors have fewer competing categories to decode.

## Test design

- Offer: One technology partner. Four ways to start.
- Channel / surface: MACS homepage + Work page
- Primary CTA: Tell us what's important
- Variable being tested: Four-bucket taxonomy and reduced outcome-first language
- Control / current version: Pre-release production homepage
- Test version: `copy/subtraction-pass-v2`
- Start date: On approved deployment
- End / review date: After enough qualified traffic/inquiries exist to compare; do not force a time window that produces noise.
- Spend cap: $0 incremental paid spend for first observation period
- Owner-time cap: Use existing site review/follow-up process
- Human approvals required: Production merge/deploy; any new public proof claim
- Stop-loss / safety rule: Revert if primary navigation/conversion path breaks or qualified inquiry completion materially drops without a compensating improvement in lead quality.
