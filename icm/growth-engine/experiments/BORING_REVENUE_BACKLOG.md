# Boring Revenue Experiment Backlog

## Purpose

This backlog converts repetitive business jobs into testable commercial experiments. It is not a product roadmap. Candidates move only when real client evidence supports them.

Rank each candidate using:

`(commercial impact x confidence x speed) / (cash cost x owner effort x complexity)`

Use the client's real baseline wherever possible.

## Default priority order

### 1. Overdue invoice recovery
Bucket: Reset
Problem: money already earned is not collected on time.
Smallest test: identify overdue invoices and send an approved escalating follow-up sequence.
Primary evidence: cash recovered, days-to-payment, owner/admin time saved.
Why first: existing money, narrow workflow, easy before/after measurement.

### 2. Lead reactivation
Bucket: Reset or Momentum
Problem: paid-for leads already exist but are cold, ignored, or unworked.
Smallest test: re-contact one historical lead segment with an approved script and booking path.
Primary evidence: re-engaged leads, qualified conversations, booked calls, sales.
Why early: uses existing acquisition spend instead of buying new demand.

### 3. Missed-call / receptionist recovery
Bucket: Reset
Problem: inbound calls arrive when nobody answers or staff are occupied.
Smallest test: cover a defined after-hours or overflow window and capture/book qualified callers.
Primary evidence: answered calls, captured leads, bookings, revenue attributable to recovered calls.

### 4. Speed-to-lead
Bucket: Reset or Scale
Problem: new leads wait too long before a useful response.
Smallest test: automate first response and qualification for one lead source.
Primary evidence: response time, contact rate, qualified appointments, conversion.

### 5. Website lead qualification
Bucket: Momentum
Problem: visitors arrive but leave without a useful next action.
Smallest test: one qualification flow tied to one primary offer.
Primary evidence: qualified conversations, booked calls, conversion from existing traffic.

### 6. Personalized outbound
Bucket: Momentum or Launch
Problem: the business needs more qualified conversations from a narrow target market.
Smallest test: one ICP, one angle, one approved outreach sequence, one CTA.
Primary evidence: positive reply rate, qualified conversations, booked calls, sales.
Guardrail: bulk outreach requires owner approval and compliance review.

### 7. Content repurposing
Bucket: Momentum
Problem: useful long-form material exists but distribution is inconsistent.
Smallest test: turn one source asset into a small approved set of channel-native posts.
Primary evidence: engaged leads, replies, profile visits that become identifiable opportunities, owner time saved.
Do not score success on impressions alone.

### 8. Inbox triage
Bucket: Reset or Scale
Problem: founder/team attention is consumed by sorting routine communication.
Smallest test: classify, label, draft routine replies, and escalate urgent items to a human.
Primary evidence: owner time saved, response time, missed-priority incidents.

### 9. Integrated lead engine
Bucket: Scale
Problem: individually working acquisition and follow-up steps are disconnected and owner-dependent.
Smallest test: integrate only components already proven in lower-level experiments.
Primary evidence: qualified pipeline, conversion, revenue/gross profit, owner time, failure rate.
Rule: this is earned through evidence. Do not start here by default.

## Experiment progression

Each candidate moves through:

1. **Observed** — real recurring problem identified.
2. **Baselined** — current cost/value measured well enough to test.
3. **Demoed** — narrow workflow works on representative data.
4. **Piloted** — used in a controlled real business slice.
5. **Proven** — commercial evidence supports continuation.
6. **Productized** — delivery, pricing, support, and evidence are repeatable.
7. **Scaled** — volume increases without unacceptable degradation.

## Stop conditions

Kill or redesign when:

- the problem is not frequent enough
- the buyer does not value the result
- attribution cannot be measured well enough
- maintenance cost overwhelms value
- human review burden remains too high
- the workflow creates unacceptable legal, financial, reputational, or customer risk
- the system depends on fabricated or vanity proof

## Evidence standard

Every experiment file must include:

- client / Client Zero label
- bucket
- problem
- baseline
- hypothesis
- smallest intervention
- approval gates
- spend
- owner/team time
- measured outputs
- failures
- proof links
- decision: kill / keep / improve / scale

No candidate becomes a public MACS promise until evidence supports the promise.
