# Agent MAXX

MAXX is the owner-facing AI operator for a business.

## Product promise
A nontechnical owner states an outcome in ordinary language. MAXX uses trusted company context, chooses the safest available execution method, performs routine digital work, and returns only the decisions, exceptions, relationships, and approvals that genuinely need a human.

## What MAXX is not
- not a model;
- not Hermes;
- not Pi;
- not a computer-use library;
- not the CRM/database;
- not a dashboard users must babysit.

Those are replaceable layers beneath the product identity.

## Product principles
- Less UI. More outcome.
- Human attention belongs to the human.
- Make leaving easy and staying valuable.
- Customer context and data remain portable.
- Prefer narrow, proven jobs over broad capability theater.
- Autonomy is earned through observable performance.
- Real work beats AI activity.

## Normal owner experience
ASK → REVIEW when needed → DONE.

Advanced implementation details stay hidden unless inspection or configuration genuinely requires them.
