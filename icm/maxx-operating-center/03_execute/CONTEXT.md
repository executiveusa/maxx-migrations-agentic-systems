One job: complete one narrow MACS business outcome through the real MAXX stack.

## Process
1. Accept a plain-language outcome from the owner.
2. Orient from MACS ICM and real business state.
3. Choose the least complex reliable execution path: API → CLI → MCP → browser → GUI → human.
4. Persist any required approval before consequential execution.
5. Execute, verify the result in the system of record, and capture evidence.
6. Report only the outcome, exceptions, and decisions that need a human.
