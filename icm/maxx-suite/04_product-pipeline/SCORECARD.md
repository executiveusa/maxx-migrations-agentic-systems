# Product / Repo Scorecard

Score each 0–5 with evidence, then decide. Scores guide discussion; they do not replace release gates.

1. Customer pain/frequency
2. AI-native advantage over conventional software
3. Nontechnical-founder simplicity
4. Unique MAXX suite leverage
5. Customer-owned data/context value
6. Open-source maintainability/license fit
7. Runtime reliability and proofability
8. Cost/latency/hardware fit
9. Distribution/GTM fit
10. Security/compliance/approval fit
11. Reuse across clients
12. Revenue or trust-building potential

Decision outputs:
- suite_role: CORE | PRODUCT | CAPABILITY | FREE | INCUBATOR | LEGACY | QUARANTINE
- engineering_posture: FORK | WRAP | REWRITE | STUDY_ONLY | DISCARD
- portfolio_action: SELL | USE | MERGE | PARK | ARCHIVE
- next_bounded_proof: one testable workflow
- owner_next_action: one plain-language decision, if any
