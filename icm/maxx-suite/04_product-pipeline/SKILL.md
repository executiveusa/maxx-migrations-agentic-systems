# MAXX Forward Product Gate

## North star

Build for where the puck is going, not where software interfaces are today.

The durable layer is not one model vendor or chatbot UI. It is the customer's portable business context, decisions, tribal knowledge, workflows, tool permissions, evaluations, evidence, distribution and trust.

AI-native means the nontechnical owner describes the outcome in normal language. The system discovers what it can, asks only material unknowns, plans, executes safe reversible work, pauses at real authority gates, proves what happened and learns from results.

## Pipeline

### 00 — OUTCOME
State the human outcome, customer, painful job, frequency and value. Ban feature-first framing.

### 01 — WHERE THE PUCK GOES
Ask:
- What part of this product becomes commodity as models improve?
- What remains scarce: proprietary context, workflow, distribution, trust, evidence, relationships, unique data or operational access?
- If voice/chat/computer-use becomes normal, which screens disappear?
- Could a small/local/open model do this job instead of an expensive frontier model?

### 02 — AI-NATIVE REFRAME
Design the workflow as:
`intent → discover context → plan → safe tool execution → approval when consequential → evidence → learn`

Do not recreate a legacy dashboard unless the user genuinely needs persistent visual state.

### 03 — NONTECHNICAL FOUNDER TEST
The owner must not need a terminal, prompt engineering, API vocabulary or system administration to get the outcome. Use one dominant next action, visible progress, plain-language errors and bounded choices.

### 04 — SUITE FIT
Choose exactly one role:
- CORE: required for the platform.
- PRODUCT: customer-facing repeatable offer.
- CAPABILITY: internal tool/subagent used by products.
- FREE: lead magnet/open giveaway that feeds the ecosystem.
- INCUBATOR: promising but unproven.
- LEGACY: salvage only.
- QUARANTINE: security/legal/brand isolation required.

### 05 — OPEN-SOURCE DUE DILIGENCE
For external/forked code choose one engineering posture:
`FORK | WRAP | REWRITE | STUDY_ONLY | DISCARD`

Check license, security, upstream activity, architecture fit, deployment cost, secret handling and whether customization will create an unmaintainable fork.

### 06 — PROOF BEFORE SCALE
Define one bounded real workflow, acceptance test, failure test, cost/latency budget, rollback and evidence receipt. No fake integrations or demo-only success claims.

### 07 — COMMERCIAL MODEL
Choose the portfolio decision:
`SELL | USE | MERGE | PARK | ARCHIVE`

Map it to the MAXX ladder without forcing every capability into a standalone SaaS. Free Agent MAXX and useful digital products may earn trust; custom high-value transformation remains separate.

### 08 — DISTRIBUTION
Define how the product is discovered: MAXX Blog, build-in-public tests, social clips, local partnerships, community work, referral, direct outreach, free product or premium diagnosis.

### 09 — GOVERNANCE
Define read/draft/write/send/publish/spend/delete authority. Consequential actions require persisted approval until explicitly narrowed by proof.

### 10 — HANDOFF
Update `REPO_REGISTRY.yaml`, the repo's `CONTEXT.md`, evidence, decision and next inspection. A memoryless agent must be able to resume without chat history.

## Rejection rules

Reject or park an idea when it:
- duplicates an existing MAXX capability without material advantage;
- makes the human manage the AI;
- depends on fabricated future integrations;
- creates another source of truth/control plane;
- has no evidence path or rollback;
- creates license/security debt disproportionate to value;
- is merely a prettier wrapper around a commodity model with no workflow/data/distribution moat.
