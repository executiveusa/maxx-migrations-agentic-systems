# MAXX Video Agent

Repo: `executiveusa/MAXX-Video-Agent`
Fork source: HKUDS/VideoAgent
Preliminary portfolio decision: `STUDY_AND_WRAP`.

Capabilities described: video understanding/Q&A, editing, commentary/overview generation, remaking, intent decomposition, graph workflow construction and self-evaluation.

## Suite role

Advanced media worker underneath MAXX, especially for understanding existing footage and constructing editing workflows.

## Risks

Heavy model/GPU/provider dependencies, broad tool surface and operational complexity. Do not make it a default customer runtime until benchmarked against simpler Clipz/FFmpeg workflows on cost, reliability and turnaround.
