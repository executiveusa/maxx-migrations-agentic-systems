# MAXX Clipz

Repo: `executiveusa/maxx-clipz` (private)
Preliminary portfolio decision: `USE` / product candidate.

Current README describes a real short-form video stack: AI clip detection, face-centered 9:16 cropping, word-synced captions, B-roll, FastAPI jobs, FFmpeg/OpenCV/MediaPipe, auth and optional SaaS billing.

## Suite role

Make this the short-form repurposing factory behind MAXX social operations and potentially a standalone paid digital product.

## Integration direction

Input: approved long-form content or source video.
Output: clips + captions + metadata + evidence/artifacts back to MAXX backend.
Distribution goes through Postiz, not directly from this repo.

## Audit before promotion

License obligations (AGPL), job reliability, provider costs, storage, caption quality, moderation, brand templates and export ownership.
