# MAXX Pacific Northwest Go-to-Market

## Position

MACS is the human company. MAXX is the product/agent ecosystem. Start with real Pacific Northwest businesses, sports/community organizations and nonprofits because proximity creates better feedback, proof and relationships—not because the product must stay geographically limited.

## Brand engines

1. **Home Team AI Lab** — build in public with Pacific Northwest/open-source technology first (for example Microsoft Phi and Ai2 OLMo), publish real business-task results, costs and failures. Never call technology vendors partners without a real relationship.
2. **MAXX Blog — Notes from the Northwest** — plain-English answers to technical problems nontechnical owners actually face.
3. **Agent MAXX** — free downloadable/portable entry product once release proof exists.
4. **MAXX Drops** — small, repeatable products that solve one real problem and do not require consulting.
5. **Premium transformation** — diagnosis-led owner-controlled systems/migrations when the problem justifies it.

## Content pillars

- subscription/tool sprawl and simplification;
- AI agents vs. chatbots, explained through owner outcomes;
- data/context ownership and portability;
- local/open model face-offs;
- sports/community/nonprofit operating systems;
- Shopify/e-commerce/UGC with Stavari;
- build-in-public Client Zero and real case studies;
- migration/escape from rented platforms.

## Commercial truth gate

The public repo currently contains a $7,500 Washington Founding Launch while recent product direction also uses a $497 diagnosis/front door. Reconcile this offer contract in `macsdigitalmedia` before paid launch. Do not run ads or automate sales copy against contradictory pricing.

## Community alignment

Track and engage with actual regional AI/community programs from Microsoft, Ai2 and other Northwest organizations. Use their open technology where licenses permit, contribute feedback, attend/support relevant community work, and pursue formal partnerships only after real mutual activity exists.
