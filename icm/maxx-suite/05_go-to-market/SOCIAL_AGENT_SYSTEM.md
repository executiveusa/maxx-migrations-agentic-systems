# MAXX AI-Native Social System

Goal: social media is operated as an evidence-driven agent workflow, not a human content treadmill.

## Agents

1. **Signal Scout** — MAXX Research scans customer questions, local/community news, Reddit/problem forums, search intent and product telemetry. Output: sourced problem signals, never fake prevalence statistics.
2. **Editorial Strategist** — maps signals to MAXX content pillars and one reader outcome.
3. **Writer** — drafts blog/post/email in plain English from approved company truth.
4. **Media Producer** — ViMax/MAXX Edits for generated/storyboard media when justified; MAXX Clipz for repurposing long-form footage.
5. **Publisher** — Postiz adapter schedules approved content and records channel/post IDs.
6. **Conversation Agent** — summarizes replies/DMs/leads and proposes responses; no spam or autonomous sensitive outreach.
7. **Analyst** — joins distribution metrics to content hypothesis and business outcome, then writes learnings back to the experiment ledger.

## Loop

`LISTEN → VERIFY → CHOOSE ONE PROBLEM → CREATE → HUMAN APPROVE → PUBLISH → MEASURE → LEARN → UPDATE ICM`

Publishing can become pre-authorized only for narrow classes after evidence proves reliability. Public claims, sensitive replies, spend and brand-policy changes remain approval gated.

## Data boundary

Postiz, video tools and research agents are execution adapters. Canonical brand truth, audience, experiments, content approvals, leads and evidence live in MAXX Migrations or authoritative connected systems.

## Output cadence direction

Prefer a small number of strong recurring series over generic daily AI content. Build-in-public experiments should create the raw material; agents repurpose one verified piece into channel-specific derivatives.
