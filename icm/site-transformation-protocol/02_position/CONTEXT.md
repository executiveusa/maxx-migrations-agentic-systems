# 02 — POSITION

## Purpose
Decide what business the website represents before deciding how it looks.

## Inputs
- Phase 01 truth artifacts
- owner-approved business facts
- ideal-customer evidence
- offer economics / engagement model
- founder/company story
- competitive and reference research

## Process
Lock the customer, painful situation, desired outcome, point of view, business model, offer architecture, founder role, trust promise, what is sold, and what stays invisible internal leverage. Name offers after client situations/outcomes rather than implementation deliverables where that is the truthful framing.

## Outputs
- `POSITIONING.md`
- `OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `MESSAGE_HIERARCHY.md`
- explicit non-goals
- approved/forbidden public claims

## Evidence
Each positioning assertion links to Phase 01 evidence or is marked as an owner DECISION/HYPOTHESIS.

## Human/authority check
Owner approves material positioning, audience, engagement model, and public promises.

## Exit condition
A memoryless agent can answer: who is this for, what situation makes them hire us, what do they buy, why us, what do we refuse to be, and which internal mechanisms must not dominate public messaging.
