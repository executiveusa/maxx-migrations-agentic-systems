# 03 — ARCHITECT

## Purpose
Define what the visitor must understand and in what order before styling begins.

## Inputs
- Phase 01 truth artifacts
- Phase 02 positioning artifacts
- named quality-bar structural research

## Process
Create the sitemap, primary and secondary navigation, visitor journeys, page hierarchy, CTA hierarchy, and low-fidelity wireframes. Every section gets one business job. Move detail deeper instead of deleting useful detail. Prefer progressive disclosure over homepage explanation.

## Outputs
- `SITEMAP.md`
- `USER_JOURNEYS.md`
- `WIREFRAMES.md`
- `CTA_HIERARCHY.md`
- page-template contracts

## Evidence
Every planned section maps to a positioning need, proof asset, user question, or conversion requirement.

## Human/authority check
Owner approves sitemap, homepage sequence, core page templates, and primary CTA before visual design.

## Exit condition
The site can be understood as boxes and words with no color, typeface, motion, or decorative design. If the hierarchy is weak in wireframe form, do not advance.
