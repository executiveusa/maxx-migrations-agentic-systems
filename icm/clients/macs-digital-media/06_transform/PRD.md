# PRD — MACS Digital Media Repositioning + Editorial Website

## Product objective

Transform `executiveusa/macsdigitalmedia` from a technical/SaaS-looking site into a selective Pacific Northwest father-and-son company whose public experience communicates through story, proof, real media, business situations, and point of view.

## Quality bar

Current live `wearecollins.com` for:
- restraint
- hierarchy
- visual confidence
- image-to-copy ratio
- editorial typography
- whitespace
- proof density
- navigation simplicity
- storytelling
- mobile behavior
- interaction quality

The goal is structural quality, not visual imitation.

## Audience

Owner-led Pacific Northwest organizations whose digital/marketing/operating systems became fragmented as the business grew. Initial focus can include established small businesses, sports/community organizations, nonprofits, service businesses, e-commerce, and local brands when evidence supports fit.

## Business model

- 90-day minimum initial engagement.
- MACS sells responsibility, judgment, transformation, output, and relationship.
- Internal software/agents are leverage behind delivery.
- Ongoing work follows demonstrated value rather than forced software lock-in.

## Brand/story architecture

- MACS Digital Media — public company and relationship.
- Stacy + Stavarai — human story and complementary operating perspectives.
- Programs — what clients buy.
- Work — proof.
- Built Here — things MACS creates/experiments with because they improve the work.
- MAXX / Buffer Blaster / Postiz / research/media agents — mechanisms and leverage, not the headline category.

## Working Programs

1. Reset — fragmented systems/vendors/trust problems.
2. Momentum — content/UGC/distribution/attention problems.
3. Scale — business has outgrown informal operations/follow-up/knowledge.
4. Launch — a new offer/business/product needs positioning, presence, distribution, and operational support.

Names remain provisional until copy lock.

## Eight implementation phases

### Phase 1 — TRUTH
Confirm repo, deployment, routes, working integrations, current copy, assets, proof, SEO, forms, analytics, APIs, and contradictions.

### Phase 2 — POSITION
Lock audience, business problem, business model, father/son story, Programs, public promises, non-goals, visible vs invisible technology.

### Phase 3 — ARCHITECT
Lock sitemap, journeys, six-beat homepage, page templates, CTA hierarchy, progressive disclosure.

### Phase 4 — PROVE
Assemble founder media, Northwest media, client work, screenshots, case-study source packs, Built Here assets, claim provenance, permissions.

Minimum proof gate before final visual build:
- one approved hero/founder media path;
- one real primary Work story;
- father + son media/story source;
- at least two Built Here assets with verified repository/product truth;
- no placeholder/fabricated proof.

### Phase 5 — DESIGN
Create the light/editorial/human MACS system: type, palette, grid, spacing, imagery, media, navigation, CTA, responsive behavior, motion/reduced-motion.

### Phase 6 — BUILD
Implement shell, homepage, templates, story, Built Here, Notes, Apply while preserving brownfield infrastructure.

### Phase 7 — GAUNTLET
Capture MACS and the named bar at equivalent viewports. Judge hero, typography, spacing, navigation, Programs, Work, story, image scale, CTA, mobile, interaction, accessibility. Iterate on the single largest gap until accepted.

### Phase 8 — LEARN
Preview proof, production approval, deployment/runtime verification, rollback, then observe and feed measured learning back into ICM.

## Non-goals

Do not produce:
- generic SaaS landing page
- AI-tool catalog
- software-dashboard aesthetic
- generic full-service agency site
- website-design-company positioning
- cheap subscription storefront
- fake case-study density
- a clone of COLLINS identity/copy/work

## Release gates

- canonical transformation walk test passed before write authority;
- proof gate passed before final design/build;
- build/lint/typecheck/tests pass;
- critical routes/forms/integrations preserved or intentionally replaced;
- responsive/accessibility/reduced-motion checks pass;
- actual Gauntlet comparison completed;
- production preview verified;
- production/domain promotion requires owner approval;
- rollback path recorded.

## Success condition

A first-time visitor can understand in seconds that MACS is a selective father-and-son Pacific Northwest company that takes responsibility for important business outcomes, can see real proof quickly, can understand the four ways to engage without learning MACS's internal stack, and can decide whether to pursue a 90-day fit conversation.
