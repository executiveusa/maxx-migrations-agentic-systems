# MACS Digital Media

## Commercial promise
MACS learns the business, identifies digital bottlenecks costing time or money, installs an owner-controlled business system, proves the result, and hands over the keys.

## Product rule
The customer is buying a better-running business, not an AI model.

## Initial audience
- sports and basketball programs;
- coaches, mentors, youth/community programs;
- minority-owned small businesses;
- adjacent nontechnical small businesses needing better lead, customer, marketing, funding, or operating systems.

## Client Zero outcome
Prove the reusable sequence:
`durable intake → ICM normalization → tenant isolation → approval-gated action → evidence → export/rollback`.

## Source precedence
1. production/runtime evidence;
2. approved current specifications;
3. owner decisions;
4. current repository implementation;
5. historical notes;
6. agent inference.
