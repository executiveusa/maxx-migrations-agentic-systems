# 01 — Intake: consent and company truth

**Inputs:** client identity and verified authorization, business owner, goals, existing tools, data owners, desired first job, baseline and budget.

**Process:** discover before asking; inventory systems, owners, source precedence, permission scopes and sensitive records. Obtain explicit consent for each data source and purpose. Choose the single money-facing workflow with the strongest evidence.

**Outputs:** approved intake record in tenant-scoped backend; source manifest with provenance; first-job definition and human approval map.

**Human gate:** founder confirms business facts, data-sharing authority, priority, permitted connections and spending ceiling.

**Exit proof:** signed consent, source inventory, baseline, one outcome and acceptance metric. No live connector or ingestion without it.
